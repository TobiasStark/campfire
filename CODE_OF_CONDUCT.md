# Code of conduct

Be decent to each other. Disagree with the code, not the person.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing other people's private information,
and repeatedly pushing a point after a maintainer has made a decision.

The maintainer can edit, hide or delete comments, and close or lock issues and pull requests that break these rules,
and can ban people who keep doing it.

To report a problem, use the private reporting link in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or contact the maintainer through
their GitHub profile.
